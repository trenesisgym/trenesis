How we write

  • Evidence first. Our guides are based on published research and the work of evidence-based coaches. Articles link to their main sources so you can check them yourself.
  • Fundamentals over hacks. Consistency, good technique, enough protein and sleep get most people most of their results. We focus on those.
  • Honest about limits. Where the evidence is unclear, we say so. Our content is general education, not medical advice.
