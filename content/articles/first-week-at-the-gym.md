Almost everyone feels out of place in their first week at the gym. The good news: nobody is watching you as closely as you think, and a little preparation removes most of the stress.

## Before you go

- **Pick a plan in advance.** Walking in without one is what makes people wander between machines. Use our [free 3-day full-body plan](full-body-workout-for-beginners.html).
- **Save the plan on your phone**, with space to write down weights and reps.
- **Bring** a water bottle, a small towel, comfortable clothes and shoes with a flat, stable sole.
- **Go at a quiet time** if you can. Early afternoon and late evening are often calmer than right after work.
- **Ask for an introduction.** Many gyms offer a free tour or a session with a trainer to show you the machines. Take it.

## Your first sessions

1. **Warm up for 5 minutes** on a bike or cross trainer, then do 1–2 light sets of your first exercise.
2. **Start lighter than you think.** For the first two weeks the goal is learning the movements, not testing your strength.
3. **Use machines first** if free weights feel intimidating. They're stable and easier to learn, and they build muscle just as well.
4. **Write everything down:** exercise, weight, reps. Next session, try to beat it by a little.
5. **Keep it short.** 45–60 minutes is plenty. Leaving wanting more is better than leaving wrecked.

## Unwritten gym rules

- Put weights back where they belong when you're done.
- Wipe down benches and machines after use.
- Don't sit on a machine scrolling your phone while others wait.
- Ask "how many sets do you have left?" or "can I work in?" if you want to share equipment. Most people are happy to.
- Don't give unasked-for advice, and don't take it personally if someone gives it to you.

## What you can ignore for now

- **Supplements.** They make very little difference until your training and eating are consistent. See [what actually works](supplements-that-work.html).
- **The perfect program.** Any reasonable plan you follow for months beats the perfect plan you quit.
- **Being sore.** Some soreness after your first sessions is normal and fades within a few days. It isn't a sign of a good or bad workout.
- **What others lift.** Everyone started somewhere, and most people are focused on themselves.

> **Key insight**
> Your only goal in the first month is to show up. Three sessions a week for four weeks builds the habit that everything else depends on.
