Some weeks there's no time for a full workout: exams, travel, a deadline. Skipping training completely breaks the habit. Two short sessions keep it going, and they keep most of your progress too.

## Why short workouts are enough for a while

Keeping strength and muscle takes far less training than building them. In one study, young adults kept their gains for months while training just one day a week, with a fraction of their usual sets. So in a busy week, the goal is simply to keep showing up.

## How it works

- Do **Workout 1 and Workout 2** once each in the week, or alternate them if you have more days.
- Exercises are paired as **supersets**: do A1, rest 30 seconds, do A2, rest 30 seconds, and repeat for 3 rounds. Then move on to the B pair.
- Start with a **3-minute warm-up**: arm circles, hip hinges and 10 easy bodyweight squats.
- Pick the version that fits what you have: bodyweight or dumbbells.

## Workout 1 (about 20 minutes)

| Pair | Bodyweight | With dumbbells | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | Split squats | Goblet squats | 8–15 (per leg for split squats) |
| A2 | Push-ups (hands raised if needed) | Floor press | 8–15 |
| B1 | Single-leg glute bridges | Romanian deadlifts | 10–15 |
| B2 | Plank | One-arm dumbbell rows | 30–45 s / 8–12 per arm |

## Workout 2 (about 20 minutes)

| Pair | Bodyweight | With dumbbells | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | Reverse lunges | Reverse lunges holding dumbbells | 8–12 per leg |
| A2 | Pike push-ups | Shoulder press | 6–12 |
| B1 | Glute bridges | Hip thrusts with a dumbbell | 12–20 |
| B2 | Dead bugs | Bent-over rows | 8–12 per side / 8–12 |

> **Key insight**
> Every exercise here is quiet: no jumping or dropping weights. That makes it apartment-friendly, even late in the evening.

## Cool-down

Short on time? One easy minute of walking and two stretches for the muscles you trained is enough. The full [5-minute cool-down routine](cool-down.html) shows which stretches go with which workout.

## When things calm down

Go back to your normal plan: the [dumbbell plan](dumbbell-workout-at-home.html), the [bodyweight plan](bodyweight-workout-plan.html) or the [gym plan](full-body-workout-for-beginners.html). You'll usually pick up close to where you left off.
