*Last updated: 31 October 2026*

We keep data collection to a minimum. This page explains what happens when you use trenesisgym.github.io/trenesis.

## Who is responsible

Volodymyr and Linus, the people behind Trenesis. Contact: [trenesisgym@gmail.com](mailto:trenesisgym@gmail.com).

## What we store on your device

- **Country choice.** If you pick a country for product recommendations, your browser saves that choice in local storage so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device and you can clear it any time in your browser settings.
- **Your plan and reading progress.** If you take the plan quiz, the result is saved in your browser so the site can show your next step. As you read articles, the site also remembers how far you got and which ones you finished, so it can show "Read" next to them. This stays on your device, is never sent to us, and you can clear it any time in your browser settings.
- **Workout logbook.** Workouts you log are saved in your browser only. They're never sent to us, and you can download or delete them at any time.
- **Calorie calculator.** Your last calculator details and result are saved in your browser, so the calculator and the home page can show them again. They never leave your device, and you can clear them any time in your browser settings.
- **Program waitlist.** If you join the waitlist for the 12-Week Program, you send us your email address, and we keep it only to tell you when the program launches. Ask us any time and we'll delete it.
- **Offline cache.** A small service worker checks for updated pages so you always see the latest version. It doesn't track you.

We don't set advertising or tracking cookies.

## Third parties involved

- **GitHub Pages** hosts the website. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Google Fonts** delivers the fonts the site uses, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google.
- **Amazon.** When you click an affiliate link you leave our site. Amazon then uses its own cookies, for example to credit the referral to us, under its own privacy notice.
- **Social media.** Links to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube take you to those platforms, which apply their own privacy policies.

If you sign up for our emails, you send us your email address. We use it only to send you Trenesis emails: new guides, training tips and news about Trenesis, including our own products once they exist. We never sell or share it.

- **How you sign up.** After you enter your address, you get one email with a link to confirm. You're only on the list once you click it. This is how we can show you agreed (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a): your consent).
- **Who stores it.** Our emails are sent with **Kit** (ConvertKit LLC, USA). Kit stores your address and, like most email services, records whether an email was opened and which links were clicked, so we can see what's useful. Kit is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which covers the transfer to the US. See [Kit's privacy policy](https://kit.com/privacy).
- **How to stop.** Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. You can also email us and we'll remove your address and delete it from Kit.
<!-- /newsletter-on -->

## Analytics

We don't use any analytics or visitor tracking yet. If we add it, we'll use a cookie-free tool that doesn't identify individual visitors, and we'll update this page first.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, and object to how we use it. Email [trenesisgym@gmail.com](mailto:trenesisgym@gmail.com). You also have the right to complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the [Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/).
