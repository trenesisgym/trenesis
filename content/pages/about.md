Trenesis is a free resource for people who want to train and eat in a way that actually works. No hype, no miracle programs, just the fundamentals explained clearly.

## Who we are

We're Volodymyr and Linus: one of us trains in Rotterdam, the other in San Francisco. When we started, we wasted months on random programs, conflicting advice and supplements we didn't need.

Trenesis is the guide we wish we'd had: the fundamentals that actually work, explained simply, for anyone starting out, wherever they train. That's also why our product picks cover the shops in your country, on both sides of the ocean.

## How we write

- **Evidence first.** Our guides are based on published research and the work of evidence-based coaches. Articles link to their main sources so you can check them yourself.
- **Fundamentals over hacks.** Consistency, good technique, enough protein and sleep get most people most of their results. We focus on those.
- **Honest about limits.** Where the evidence is unclear, we say so. Our content is general education, not medical advice.

## How we make money

Some product pages contain affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases, at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links are always labelled.
