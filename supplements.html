Protein powder

Consider for
Convenience when you want a protein option

Treat powder as a food choice to compare with the other protein sources in your routine. Convenience, taste and dietary preferences all matter.

What to look for

Compare protein per serving, ingredients, allergens, serving size and cost per equivalent amount of protein. For a plant-based option, check the full ingredient list.

The trade-off

A bigger tub or scoop does not necessarily mean better value. Whey is milk-derived; “plant-based” still requires an allergen check.

Creatine monohydrate

Consider for
Understanding a supplement before choosing it

Read the evidence and suitability information first. If you decide to use creatine, compare clearly labeled products rather than marketing claims.

What to look for

Look for the listed form, amount per serving, ingredient transparency and independent testing you can verify.

The trade-off

A blend may include ingredients you do not want. A premium name alone does not establish better quality.
