Fat loss without starving: 5 meal tips and example days

Practical ways to stay in a calorie deficit without feeling miserable, simple food swaps, and example fat-loss days for meat-eaters and plant-based eaters.

3 min read · Updated October 2026

Knowing you need a calorie deficit is easy. Sticking to one without feeling miserable is the hard part. These tips make it easier.

1. Eat protein at every meal

High-protein meals keep you full longer and reduce cravings. If every meal has a solid protein source (eggs, chicken, tofu, cottage cheese, Greek yoghurt), staying within your calories gets much easier.

2. Use lower-calorie swaps

Instead of Try
Regular yoghurt Greek yoghurt or cottage cheese (more protein, fewer calories)
Juice or smoothies Whole fruit (far more filling per calorie)
A big plate of white rice A smaller portion plus extra vegetables
Creamy sauces Tomato- or yoghurt-based sauces
Soft drinks Zero-sugar versions (sweeteners are fine in moderation)

3. Consider a protein shake for breakfast

A shake gives about 25 g of protein for roughly 120 kcal. Compared with a 400–600 kcal breakfast, that frees up a lot of calories for the rest of the day. It's one of the simplest ways to create a comfortable deficit, if it suits you.

4. Plan for hunger, not just meals

Decide in advance what you'll eat when you're hungry between meals: a boiled egg, a rice cake, a small pot of cottage cheese. A ready answer stops impulse choices when willpower is low.

5. Don't skip meals to make up for overeating

Skipping a meal after eating too much usually backfires: you get hungrier and overeat later. Have a smaller, protein-rich meal instead.

Key insight

You don't need to be perfect. Going over your target one day isn't a problem. Think in weekly averages, not daily perfection.

Example days

These examples are deliberately simple and on the low side: most adults need more calories than this. Use them as a template and scale portions to your own calorie target.

Example day

Meal Example ~Calories ~Protein
Breakfast Protein shake (1 scoop whey + water) 120 kcal 25 g
Lunch Chicken breast + rice + broccoli 520 kcal 45 g
Snack Cottage cheese (150 g) + cucumber 140 kcal 17 g
Dinner Salmon + sweet potato + green beans 500 kcal 38 g
Total 1,280 kcal 125 g

Plant-based example day

Meal Example ~Calories ~Protein
Breakfast Plant protein shake (1 scoop + water) 120 kcal 25 g
Lunch Spiced lentils + brown rice + spinach 480 kcal 25 g
Snack Soy yoghurt (150 g) + berries 130 kcal 11 g
Dinner Tofu stir-fry + broccoli + cauliflower rice 420 kcal 30 g
Total 1,150 kcal 91 g

Always hungry in a deficit?

Eat more protein and fibre: both are very filling. Adding vegetables, legumes and a protein source to every meal makes a big difference for the same calories. A glass of water before meals helps too.
