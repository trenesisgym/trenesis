Shaker bottles

Consider for
Mixing on the go

A simple bottle with an effective seal may be all you need. Choose a material and size that fit how you use it.

What to look for

Check cleaning access, lid design, capacity and dishwasher instructions. A wide opening makes everyday cleaning easier.

The trade-off

Insulated steel costs more and can be heavier. A more expensive bottle is not necessarily easier to clean.

Lifting straps

Consider for
When grip limits a planned pulling exercise

Straps are a specific tool for pulling movements. Decide whether they address a limitation in your own training before adding them.

What to look for

Compare strap length, material, stitching and how easily you can release the strap. Learn the intended setup before using it with a load.

The trade-off

They add setup time and are not a substitute for learning the movement or deciding how to train your grip.

Grip trainers

Consider for
Dedicated grip practice

A grip trainer is an optional addition if focused grip work is part of your plan. You do not need one simply because you lift.

What to look for

Check handle comfort, resistance range and whether the adjustment mechanism is secure.

The trade-off

A hand gripper’s movement is different from holding a loaded bar. Choose a tool that matches the task you want to practice.
