Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

We keep data collection to a minimum. This page explains what happens when you use trenesisgym.github.io/trenesis.

Who is responsible

Names, or business name and address once registered with the KvK. Contact: contact email.

What we store on your device

  • Country choice. If you pick a country for product recommendations, your browser saves that choice in local storage so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device and you can clear it any time in your browser settings.
  • Offline cache. A small service worker checks for updated pages so you always see the latest version. It doesn't track you.

We don't set advertising or tracking cookies.

Third parties involved

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts delivers the fonts the site uses, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google.
  • Amazon. When you click an affiliate link you leave our site. Amazon then uses its own cookies, for example to credit the referral to us, under its own privacy notice.
  • Social media. Links to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube take you to those platforms, which apply their own privacy policies.

Email newsletter

Add once the email tool is chosen: what we collect (email address, optionally first name), which provider stores it, that we only use it to send the guides and our newsletter, and that every email has an unsubscribe link.

Analytics

Add once analytics is set up: which tool, that it's cookie-free and doesn't identify individual visitors.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, and object to how we use it. Email contact email. You also have the right to complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
