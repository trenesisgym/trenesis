Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

We keep data collection to a minimum. This page explains what happens when you use trenesisgym.github.io/trenesis.

Who is responsible

Volodymyr and Linus, the people behind Trenesis. Contact: trenesisgym@gmail.com.

What we store on your device

  • Country choice. If you pick a country for product recommendations, your browser saves that choice in local storage so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device and you can clear it any time in your browser settings.
  • Your plan and reading progress. If you take the plan quiz, the result is saved in your browser so the site can show your next step. As you read articles, the site also remembers how far you got and which ones you finished, so it can show "Read" next to them. This stays on your device, is never sent to us, and you can clear it any time in your browser settings.
  • Workout logbook. Workouts you log are saved in your browser only. They're never sent to us, and you can download or delete them at any time.
  • Program waitlist. If you join the waitlist for the 12-Week Program, you send us your email address, and we keep it only to tell you when the program launches. Ask us any time and we'll delete it.
  • Offline cache. A small service worker checks for updated pages so you always see the latest version. It doesn't track you.

We don't set advertising or tracking cookies.

Third parties involved

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts delivers the fonts the site uses, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google.
  • Amazon. When you click an affiliate link you leave our site. Amazon then uses its own cookies, for example to credit the referral to us, under its own privacy notice.
  • Social media. Links to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube take you to those platforms, which apply their own privacy policies.

Email newsletter

We don't run a newsletter yet. Before we start one, we'll update this page to explain what we collect, which service stores it and how to unsubscribe.

Analytics

We don't use any analytics or visitor tracking yet. If we add it, we'll use a cookie-free tool that doesn't identify individual visitors, and we'll update this page first.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, and object to how we use it. Email trenesisgym@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
