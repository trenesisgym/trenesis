How to gain weight when you're never hungry: meal tips and example days

Easy ways to eat a small calorie surplus when you struggle to gain weight, example 3,000 kcal days for meat-eaters and plant-based eaters, and a protein source table.

3 min read · Updated October 2026

Some people struggle to lose weight; others struggle to gain it. If you're in the second group, these tips make a small surplus much easier.

1. Drink your calories

Liquid calories are much easier to get down than solid food when you're not hungry. A shake can add 400–700 kcal in a few minutes without feeling like a heavy meal.

Simple shake: whole milk + 2 scoops protein powder + a banana + a tablespoon of peanut butter = roughly 600–700 kcal and 50–60 g of protein.

2. Don't forget to eat

It sounds obvious, but many people who can't gain simply skip meals because they're busy or not hungry. Set reminders if you need to. Three meals plus two snacks is a solid baseline.

3. Add calorie-dense extras to meals you already eat

You don't need much more food, just denser additions: a drizzle of olive oil, a handful of nuts, a spoon of peanut butter.

4. Don't overdo it

"More is better" doesn't apply here. A big surplus mostly adds fat, not faster muscle; your body can only build muscle so fast. Keep the surplus to about 200–300 kcal a day, which works out to roughly 0.25 kg a week, or about 1 kg a month.

5. Spread meals across the day

If big portions are hard, 4–5 manageable meals beat one huge dinner to make up for a light day.

Key insight

Progress is slow on purpose. If the scale hasn't moved after 2–3 weeks, add one snack or slightly bigger portions. Don't overthink it.

Example days

Starting points around 3,000 kcal. Adjust portions to your own target.

Example day

Meal Example ~Calories ~Protein
Breakfast Oats (100 g) + whey + whole milk + banana 650 kcal 35 g
Snack Shake: whole milk + 2 scoops whey + peanut butter 680 kcal 55 g
Lunch Minced beef + pasta + tomato sauce 580 kcal 35 g
Snack Mixed nuts + Greek yoghurt (150 g) 420 kcal 20 g
Dinner Chicken thighs + rice + olive oil 700 kcal 45 g
Total 3,030 kcal 190 g

Plant-based example day

Meal Example ~Calories ~Protein
Breakfast Oats (100 g) + plant protein + almond butter + banana 650 kcal 38 g
Snack Shake: oat milk + 2 scoops plant protein + peanut butter 620 kcal 50 g
Lunch Chickpea and brown rice bowl + tahini + roasted veg 580 kcal 35 g
Snack Mixed nuts + soy yoghurt (150 g) 400 kcal 18 g
Dinner Tempeh + rice + stir-fried greens + sesame oil 680 kcal 42 g
Total 2,930 kcal 183 g

Not hungry enough to hit your target?

Lean on calorie-dense foods and liquid calories instead of forcing bigger plates: smoothies, whole milk, nut butters and olive oil are your most efficient tools.
