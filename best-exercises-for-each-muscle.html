The best exercises for each muscle (with form tips)

A simple menu of proven gym exercises for chest, back, arms, shoulders, abs and legs, with short notes on when to pick each one.

2 min readUpdated October 2026

You don't need dozens of exercises. Pick one or two per muscle from the lists below, plug them into your split and focus on getting stronger at them.

Chest

Exercise Notes
Bench press Use a spotter or safety arms.
Dumbbell bench press Less stable, so harder to learn than a machine press.
Machine chest press Stable and beginner-friendly.
Flyes (dumbbell, cable or machine) Good for isolating the chest.

Back

Exercise Target Notes
Lat pulldown (wide or close grip) Lats
Close-grip cable row Lats Pull toward your belly button, not your chest.
Wide-grip cable row Upper back Pull toward your chest and squeeze your shoulder blades at the end.
T-bar row Upper back Pull toward your chest and squeeze your shoulder blades.
Kelso shrugs Upper back A good choice if you feel rows mostly in your biceps.
Back extension Lower back

Arms

Exercise Target Notes
Dumbbell curls Biceps Great if you only have dumbbells.
Preacher curls Biceps Very stable, easy to focus on the muscle.
Hammer curls Biceps A bit more brachialis work.
Cable triceps pushdown Triceps
Overhead extension Triceps Trains the long head in a stretched position.
Reverse curls Forearms Emphasises the brachioradialis.
Wrist curls and extensions Forearms

Shoulders

Exercise Notes
Lateral raises (cable, dumbbell or machine) Mainly the side delts: what makes shoulders look wider.
Shoulder press Front delts and triceps.
Reverse flyes Great for the rear delts.

Abs

Exercise Notes
Crunches (machine, cable or bodyweight) Actually round your spine instead of just bending at the hips.
Leg raises Same cue: curl your pelvis up rather than only lifting your legs.

Legs

Exercise Target Notes
Squats Quads, glutes
Leg press Quads or glutes Feet lower for more quads, higher for more glutes.
Leg extension Quads Hits the rectus femoris, which squats and leg press train less.
Leg curl Hamstrings
Hip thrusts Glutes Great for isolating the glutes.
Standing calf raise Calves Likely better for calf growth than seated raises.
Seated calf raise Calves
