How the 12 weeks are built

Each phase has one job, so you always know why you’re doing what you’re doing.

  1. Weeks 1–4

    Learn the lifts

    Moderate weights, 2–3 reps away from failure. You learn the movements and find your starting weights.

  2. Weeks 5–8

    Get stronger

    More sets and closer to failure. The logbook moves your weights up every week or two.

  3. Week 9

    Easy week

    Half the sets, same weights. Your body catches up, and you start the last block fresh.

  4. Weeks 10–12

    Push and test

    Your hardest weeks, then a test in week 12 so you see exactly how far you’ve come.

A logbook that thinks for you

Enter your weights and reps after each set. The logbook compares them with last time and tells you what to lift next, so you never have to guess whether to go heavier.
